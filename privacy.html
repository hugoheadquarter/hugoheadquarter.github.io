LingoScroll Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026

LingoScroll helps you learn Korean through short scenes. This policy says what happens to your information when you use the app.

Your answers stay on your phone

The questions you answer when you start (like your goals, your level and your age range) are saved on your phone. We use them only to pick your scenes and set up your feed. LingoScroll has no accounts and no servers of its own, so your answers aren't sent to us. We never sell them, and we never share them.

Your progress and settings in the app are saved on your phone too.

No ads, no tracking

LingoScroll has no ads, and it doesn't track you. It doesn't collect information about you or your device. Apart from purchases, which Apple handles, nothing you do in the app leaves your phone.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app whether you're subscribed.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, they're scheduled on your phone. You can turn them off anytime in your iPhone's Settings.

Children

LingoScroll isn't meant for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date.

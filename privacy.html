Lingo Scroll Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

Lingo Scroll helps you learn Korean through short scenes. This policy says what happens to your information when you use the app.

Your answers stay on your phone

The questions you answer when you start (like your goals, your level and your age range) are saved on your phone. We use them only to pick your scenes and set up your feed. Lingo Scroll has no accounts and no servers of its own, so your answers aren't sent to us. We never sell them, and we never send them to ad networks.

Your progress and settings in the app are saved on your phone too.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app whether you're subscribed.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, they're scheduled on your phone. You can turn them off anytime in your iPhone's Settings.

Ads in the free version

If you don't subscribe, you can unlock a scene by choosing to watch an ad. The ads come from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, limit how often you see them, and prevent fraud, Google may collect information from your device, such as:

  • your IP address and the rough location it shows,
  • device and app information, and identifiers for your device,
  • your advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking,
  • how you interact with the ads, and crash and performance data.

Google's own policy explains how it uses this information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Subscribers don't see ads.

Your choices

  • Tracking. Before your first ad, your iPhone asks whether Lingo Scroll may track you. You can change your answer anytime in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland. Before your first ad, we ask for your consent through Google's consent form. You can change your choices anytime: open a scene, tap Aa, then Privacy choices.
  • Under 18. If you tell us you're under 18, we ask Google to treat your ad requests as coming from a teen: ads are family-safe and never personalized, and the app doesn't ask to track you.

Children

Lingo Scroll isn't meant for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date.

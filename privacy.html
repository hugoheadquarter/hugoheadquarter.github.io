LingoScroll Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

LingoScroll helps you learn Korean through short video episodes. This policy says what information the app collects, why, and what you can do about it.

The short version

  • LingoScroll has no accounts. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • The app sends us information about how it's used, linked to a random ID made when you install it. We use it to choose your episodes and to make the app better.
  • If you tell us what you'd like to watch, a person on our team reads it, and we may make an episode from it. That episode can be shown to other people too, with nothing about you in it.
  • We never sell your information and never use it for ads. We share it only with the services that run the app for us, which are listed below.
  • LingoScroll has no ads and doesn't track you across other apps or websites.

What the app collects

  • Your answers. The questions you answer when you start: why you're learning, what you like to watch, your goals, your level, your age range, your gender (or "rather not say"), how you heard about LingoScroll, what's been hard, how much time you have, and when you'd like a reminder.
  • What you watch. Which episodes were shown to you, how long each one played, where you left it, the words you tapped, and the episodes you liked.
  • What you ask for. If you type what you'd like to watch, we keep your words. Please don't put personal details in a request (names, phone numbers, addresses).
  • How you use the app. The screens you see, the buttons you tap, your text settings and your reminder settings.
  • Screen recordings. Recordings of the app's own screens while you use it, showing what the app showed and where you tapped. The box where you type a request is hidden in them, and screens that aren't the app's (like Apple's purchase sheet) aren't recorded.
  • Your subscription. Which plan you chose, your free trial, renewals, cancellations and refunds, and an ID Apple gives the app for your purchases. We never see your name, your Apple Account or your payment details.
  • Your device and the app. The model of your iPhone or iPad, screen size, system version, app version, language, region and time zone, whether you're on Wi-Fi or mobile data, whether sound plays through the speaker or headphones and how loud, whether notifications are on, crashes and errors, and how fast the app starts and its videos load.
  • Approximate location. Your city and country, worked out from your internet (IP) address. Never your precise location.

All of this is linked to the app's random ID for your install, not to your name or Apple Account.

Why

To choose your episodes: your answers, what you watch and what you like decide what comes next in your feed. To make what you asked for. To see what works and what doesn't: which episodes people enjoy, where they stop, what to make next, and how the subscription is doing. And to find and fix bugs and crashes.

When you ask for an episode

The app may ask what you'd like to watch. Answering is up to you: you can leave the box empty and carry on.

What you type is sent to our server and to our team's own chat on Telegram, where a person reads it and decides whether to make it. To make an episode we use two AI services: Anthropic's reads your words to help plan and write the scene, and BytePlus's generates the pictures and the video from a description we write. Neither gets your ID or anything else about you.

The finished episode becomes part of LingoScroll, and other people may see it. Nothing in it says who asked for it.

Who handles it

  • PostHog, Inc. stores the information about how the app is used, on servers in the United States.
  • Supabase, Inc. runs our database and server, in the United States: your answers, what you watched and liked, and what you asked for.
  • Cloudflare, Inc. delivers the videos to your phone. Like any website, it sees your internet (IP) address when your phone fetches a file.
  • Telegram carries the requests to our team's chat.
  • Anthropic and BytePlus provide the AI services described above.
  • Apple handles purchases and subscriptions.

Each of them handles the information only to provide its service to us. We don't share your information with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Screen recordings are kept for 90 days. Everything else is kept for up to 7 years, or until you ask us to delete it. An episode made from a request stays in LingoScroll; your words and your ID are deleted with the rest of your data.

Your choices

  • See or delete your data. Use Contact us in the app (on the Profile tab). The email it starts includes your app's ID, which is how we find your data. Ask us, and we'll send you what we have or delete it.
  • Requests. You never have to type one.
  • Reminders. If you turn on reminders, they're scheduled on your phone. You can turn them off on the app's Profile tab or in your device's Settings.

If you're in the European Union or the United Kingdom: we use your answers, what you watch and what you ask for to provide the app to you, and the rest because we have a legitimate interest in understanding and improving the app. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it, and you can complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

LingoScroll isn't meant for children under 13.

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date.
